Thirty-nine days, and the app could not say it before: it stored the day a bill is due and not the day it closes, and the interest-free period Bank Negara requires runs from the statement date. Twenty days minimum, and only while nothing is carried forward. The BSN row stays blank rather than guessing — one imported statement would fill it, and until then the comparison is not the app’s to make.

Reading the marks
Money leaves — an amount and a date
Money arrives
A bill closes — nothing moves

The hollow ring is the whole point of the distinction. A statement closing is the most consequential day in a card’s month and the only one on which nothing at all happens to your money. The 8th decides what the 28th costs.
