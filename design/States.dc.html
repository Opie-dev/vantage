Welcome to Vantage.
Three ways to get your data in:
1 Run the OpenD sync — python sync/moomoo_sync.py
2 Add instruments and log trades under Positions
3 Record a deposit under Wallet
First run. Shown on the Dashboard when there are no transactions and no cash rows at all.
Empty states are per-screen and specific. Positions says "sync from OpenD or add a BUY"; History names the filter that matched nothing and offers to clear it; Instruments says nothing is held; the equity curve explains it fills in as snapshots accumulate. None of them is a generic "no data".
