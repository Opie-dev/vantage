The conversion is the whole reason this table exists. A car loan quoted at 3.40% flat charges interest on the original amount for the entire term, so its true cost is 1.8× the number on the contract — and it sits above ASB, not below it. Malaysian car loans are quoted flat and savings are quoted effective; putting them in one column without converting would be the exact mistake this feature exists to prevent. Vantage states the rates in one unit and stops there — what to do about them is not its call. The conversion is an IRR solved against the payment schedule, not a shortcut formula: it reproduces the effective rates Malaysian banks publish beside their own flat rates to the basis point.
