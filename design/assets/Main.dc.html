EPF does not follow the rule the other two do. ASB and Tabung Haji pay on the mean of twelve monthly minimums; EPF uses a modified aggregate daily balance, where each month’s contribution earns from the last day of that month to 31 December. So a January contribution earns nearly a full year of dividend and a December one earns almost none — the same timing lesson, reached by different arithmetic. This is what rate_basis is a column for.
